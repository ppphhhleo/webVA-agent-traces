Trace-level evidence

Evidence faithfulness and action visibility

More visible work does not necessarily produce more faithful evidence.

Each small point is one coded trial-one trace and opens that trace when selected. Horizontal position is the share of working rounds performed on screen; answer-only traces with no working actions appear at 100% visible by convention. Points receive deterministic, density-aware jitter within their evidence band to reveal overlap, while model means and confidence regions use the unjittered values. Color encodes each model using a colorblind-safe palette. Evidence categories form a descriptive ordered scale—not an answer-correctness score. Select a model label or mean point to focus its traces.

Trace-level detail

Off-screen reliance and delay

Each point is one trace. Points farther right rely more on off-screen work; higher points delay that shift longer. Traces that never go off-screen are packed at the upper-left corner.

Metric definition

A shared baseline across traces

Vertical axis

First off-screen round ÷ all rounds. Round 1 of a 30-round trace is 3.3%; never is positioned at 100%.

Horizontal axis

Off-screen rounds ÷ (GUI + off-screen rounds). GUI share is the complement to 100%.

Round classification

Desktop actions, screenshots, and waits are GUI. Shell, Python, file, and browser-script work are off-screen.
