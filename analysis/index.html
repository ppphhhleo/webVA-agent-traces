Interpretation

Behavioral signatures

These signatures describe interaction strategy—not equivalent evidence of human-like reasoning or answer grounding.

GPT-5.4 Interface-bound persistence

Only 11.5% of its working rounds are off screen, and 19 of 30 traces never leave the interface. Its mean switch latency is 87.2%—the slowest of the four—although visible persistence does not always produce the right answer.

GPT-5.5 Backend-first extraction

It spends 66.2% of working rounds off screen, starts there in 18 of 30 traces, and has the lowest mean switch latency at 41.5%—the fastest switch. A characteristic run searches bundles, opens Chrome remote debugging, and parses recovered data in code.

Claude Opus 4.8 Task-adaptive switching

Its off-screen share rises from 19.2% on low-level tasks to 52.0% on compound tasks, while its overall mean switch latency is 70.1%. It often begins visually, then moves to Python or shell when exact aggregation is useful.

Trace-level detail

Off-screen reliance and delay

Each point is one trace. Points farther right rely more on off-screen work; higher points delay that shift longer. Traces that never go off-screen are packed at the upper-left corner.

Metric definition

A shared baseline across traces

Vertical axis

First off-screen round ÷ all rounds. Round 1 of a 30-round trace is 3.3%; never is positioned at 100%.

Horizontal axis

Off-screen rounds ÷ (GUI + off-screen rounds). GUI share is the complement to 100%.

Round classification

Desktop actions, screenshots, and waits are GUI. Shell, Python, file, and browser-script work are off-screen.
