Coded behavior overview

Behavior prevalence and frequency

Bar length and percentage show trace prevalence; n reports coded occurrences.

For each model and behavior, prevalence is the share of that model’s 30 traces containing at least one instance of the code. Frequency counts every coded occurrence, so it can exceed the number of affected traces when a behavior recurs. The matrix is derived reactively from the trial-one behavior annotation file.

Reading the quadrants

Visibility changes the cost of checking—not whether evidence is faithful

Upper left · less visible, evidence-backed

Costly to audit

The claim has a computed or mixed evidentiary basis, but reviewers must leave the visible interface and reconstruct off-screen work to verify it.

  • tr_c953e1cb79849f1fOpus bypasses Data Voyager, computes correlations and means in code, and never reconciles them with the view.
  • tr_885beba33a3c2e3fAfter a failed brush, Opus downloads and analyzes the wine data off screen; the computed answer is grounded but laborious to inspect.

Upper right · more visible, evidence-backed

Readily inspectable

The decisive evidence is established in the rendered interface, making the connection between action, visible state, and claim relatively cheap to check.

  • tr_ac736c74cc47e0c9GPT‑5.5 reads its Gapminder claim directly from a hovered bubble, tying the answer to a visible tooltip.
  • tr_3c23536d65984f39Despite a repeated hover loop, Opus compares the visible Vitessce panels and grounds its conclusion in the displayed groupings.

Lower left · less visible, unsupported

Opaque and untrustworthy

Off-screen work neither establishes reliable evidence nor leaves an inspectable visual account, so both the process and its conclusion are difficult to trust.

  • tr_8c9eccf85eb1f0ebGPT‑5.5 misclicks the sort control, searches bundles off screen, then reports an incorrect ranking from an unestablished interface state.
  • tr_4c29dca0e244dc14Sonnet alternates between LineUp and failed engineering attempts until the 100-round cap, without obtaining or delivering an answer.

Lower right · more visible, unsupported

Visible activity, misplaced trust

The interaction is easy to observe, but the final claim is not supported by the visible result; apparent transparency can therefore create false confidence.

  • tr_475d111272ead831Sonnet spends 93 rounds micro-reaiming at an unresponsive Vitessce target, then fabricates the comparison it claims to have observed.
  • tr_6803ce072cf0a6e8Opus performs prolonged visible hovering in Gapminder, but substitutes prior knowledge for evidence in the final exception claim.

Trace-level detail

Off-screen reliance and delay

Each point is one trace. Points farther right rely more on off-screen work; higher points delay that shift longer. Traces that never go off-screen are packed at the upper-left corner.

Metric definition

A shared baseline across traces

Vertical axis

First off-screen round ÷ all rounds. Round 1 of a 30-round trace is 3.3%; never is positioned at 100%.

Horizontal axis

Off-screen rounds ÷ (GUI + off-screen rounds). GUI share is the complement to 100%.

Round classification

Desktop actions, screenshots, and waits are GUI. Shell, Python, file, and browser-script work are off-screen.
